## Definition

**BEM ($BEM)** is a protocol token discussed alongside Proof of Design (PoD) mining and fee mechanisms.

## How it works

Eligible circuits may participate under publicly disclosed PoD rules. Weights, task banks, and fees follow official PoD documentation.

## Boundaries / non-claims

- Mining does not require buying $BEM; official materials also warn that shallow liquidity can move price sharply.
- Price and holder distribution are observational topics for tapeout.work, not encyclopedia promises.
