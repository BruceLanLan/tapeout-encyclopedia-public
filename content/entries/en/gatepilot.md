## Definition
**GatePilot** (guide URL: tapeout.vibedegens.com) offers gate-level optimization and design search, and has expanded into **miner leasing**.
## How it works
Design helpers reduce trial-and-error; leasing usually requires approving a circuit NFT. Before approving, check upgradeability, allowance scope, and domain match.
## Boundaries / non-claims
- Not the official mining UI.
- Leasing/delegation is a high-risk approval scenario — see [wallet-safety](wallet-safety.md).
