## Definition

**Canvas** is the official circuit design tool: wire and simulate in the browser without burning transistors or writing to chain.

## How it works

Drop NAND / LATCH, connect wires, simulate freely; tape-out only when satisfied.

## Boundaries / non-claims

- A successful simulation is not an on-chain circuit.
- Verify the full official domain before connecting a wallet.
