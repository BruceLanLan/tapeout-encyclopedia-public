## Definition
An operational **wallet safety checklist** for the TapeOut ecosystem: verify public materials before signing or approving.
## How it works
1. Verify the full domain (official entry vs community tool).
2. Read the approval: which asset, unlimited?, upgradeable contract?
3. Revoke approvals after using third-party tools (block-explorer approval checkers help).
4. Ignore DMs offering to “build miners” or operate for you.
5. Before selling a circuit/miner, confirm pending rewards are claimed (per the guide).
6. If a wallet warns “phishing” on a very new site, first verify the domain is truly official/known — warnings can also mean the allowlist is incomplete.
## Boundaries / non-claims
- A checklist does not replace your own on-chain checks.
- No guarantee of asset safety.
