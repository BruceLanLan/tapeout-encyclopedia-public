## Definition

**TapeOut** is a protocol on BNB Chain that turns logic gates into tokens so people can assemble circuits and processors on-chain. The public site frames it as on-chain hardware manufacturing infrastructure.

The name borrows semiconductor “tape-out”: after designing on a browser canvas, transistor tokens are burned and a circuit NFT is minted. This is unrelated to fabricating silicon chips.

## How it works

Asset chain (pedagogical):

**Transistor → Canvas → Tape Out → Circuit → Processor → Container**

NAND handles combinational logic; LATCH holds state. Canvas simulation is free and off-chain; tape-out burns transistors and is irreversible.
