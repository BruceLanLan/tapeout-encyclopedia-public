## Definition
**TapeChat** (chat.tapeout.link) is described in the beginner guide as an on-chain messaging tool by Xiao_zhanyang (@Xiao_zhanyang), built as a TapeSend-based client, with support for cross-sending among BNB Chain, X Layer, and Base (per the release post cited in ch.8).
## How it works
It sits on the TapeSend / TAP-10 messaging layer between circuit containers. Verify the domain before connecting a wallet; client UI and chain coverage can change independently of the protocol.
## Boundaries / non-claims
- Not an official client.
- Capabilities and risks follow the third-party implementation, not TapeSend alone.
