## Definition

**PoD (Proof of Design)** is TapeOut’s disclosed design-proof mechanism: under public rules, eligible circuit designs participate in related issuance flows.

## How it works

Official materials separate read-only wallet connection from irreversible signed actions. Task banks and parameters follow official pages; this encyclopedia only navigates and states boundaries.
