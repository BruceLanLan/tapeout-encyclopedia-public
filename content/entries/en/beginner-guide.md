## Definition

The *TapeOut Beginner Complete Guide* is a structured onboarding textbook split into collaborative Markdown with page-level provenance. Online entry: [TapeOut Daily](https://tapeoutdaily.ai/learn/guide).

## How it works

Its contribution rules forbid expanding beyond the original PDF — ideal for a stable textbook, not an encyclopedia. This project cites chapters/pages instead of rewriting that repo.

## Boundaries / non-claims

- New encyclopedia entries do not land in the textbook repo body.
- Collaboration request draft: `docs/COLLABORATION_REQUEST.md`.
