## Definition
**Spoof / same-name tokens** share a ticker or brand string but not the disclosed contract. The ecosystem map also flags unrelated same-name projects.
## How it works
- The guide highlights recognizing the BEM address in truncated form (`0x5ce0…695a`) — always verify the **full address** from official channels and a block explorer; never trust the name alone.
- `$TapeOut`-like tickers on other chains may be unrelated.
- Fake pools, airdrop DMs, and “claim” links are common delivery channels.
## Boundaries / non-claims
- This encyclopedia is not a complete allowlist; use official disclosures plus your own verification.
