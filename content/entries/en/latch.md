## Definition

**LATCH** is TapeOut’s latch / one-bit memory transistor token. Without state cells, sequential logic and richer processor behavior cannot be expressed.

## How it works

Like NAND, LATCH can be simulated for free on Canvas and is consumed on tape-out. Issuance and balances follow the relevant processor contracts and official UI.

## Boundaries / non-claims

- Holding LATCH is not a yield promise.
