## Definition
**TAPQQ** is listed in the beginner guide as a retro QQ window-style on-chain chat app at `tape://1.888` (gateway: 1-888.tapekit.org). Chapter 8.9 marks it as the guide editor’s own work (not an official product), currently peer-to-peer chat.
## How it works
Open via TapeKit / a tape:// gateway. The guide notes a common mix-up with `1688-0.tapekit.org` (BURNBEM arcade); TAPQQ itself is `tape://1.888`. Prefer a fresh wallet when trying editor demos.
## Boundaries / non-claims
- Community / editor work — not TapeOut official.
- Early / incomplete; verify domain and signatures before use.
