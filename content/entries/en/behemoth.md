## Definition

**Behemoth** is the official-site name for processor #1; the guide notes on-chain names such as Blonskr_No1 and discusses it with mining coefficients.

## How it works

Which processors qualify for PoD and how coefficients work follow official PoD / rules pages. Coefficients in older articles may be stale.

## Boundaries / non-claims

- Names and mining eligibility can change; prefer current official disclosures and observation APIs.
