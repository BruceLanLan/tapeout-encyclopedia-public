## Definition

**Tape-out** commits a canvas design on-chain: transistors used in the design are burned and a circuit NFT is minted. The action is irreversible.

## How it works

Unrelated to fabricating silicon — only contract state changes. After tape-out, logic lives on-chain as data and can be called.

## Boundaries / non-claims

- Confirm transistor balances and the design before signing; there is no routine “undo tape-out”.
