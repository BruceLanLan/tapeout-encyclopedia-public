## Definition
**TapeStar** (tapestar.org) is described in beginner-guide ch.8 as a “one-click mining” aggregator by the MantanCoin (@mantancoin) team (intro post 2026-09-23, early Beta): it compares buy-materials and tape-out plans by budget and claims assets stay in your own wallet.
## How it works
Aggregation flows typically involve signatures. The guide advises a small trial on first use. Verify domain, upgradeability of any approved contracts, and allowance scope — see [wallet-safety](wallet-safety.md).
## Boundaries / non-claims
- Not official.
- Early Beta; self-descriptions about non-custody are claims to verify, not encyclopedia guarantees.
- No yield or mining-success promises.
