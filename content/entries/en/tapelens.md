## Definition
**TapeLens** (tapelens.xyz) is listed in beginner-guide ch.8 under mining helpers as a viewer for circuit and miner data.
## How it works
Useful for inspecting circuit / miner-related displays before or alongside other tools. Treat any connect-wallet or approval flow with the usual domain and allowance checks.
## Boundaries / non-claims
- Not official.
- Data presentation is third-party; cross-check important facts on-chain or via other explorers.
