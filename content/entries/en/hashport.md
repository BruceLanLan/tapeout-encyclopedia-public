## Definition

**HashPort** is listed in the beginner guide as a TapeKit-based commercial hosting service for uploading sites into containers; founder commentary has described it as an early commercialization project.

## How it works

Read its terms and permission prompts before use; uploads imply authorization flows that can change container contents.

## Boundaries / non-claims

- A commercial service is not the core protocol contracts.
- This encyclopedia does not endorse its security or pricing.
