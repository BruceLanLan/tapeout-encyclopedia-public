## Definition
**tapeout.space** is described in the beginner guide (author notes by SpongeMochi / @spongemochi) as an on-chain “circuit-map” site, community gateway, and one-click publish tooling. The author states it is a community gateway, not an official entry, and can open sites that are not yet provisioned elsewhere.
## How it works
Use it as a navigation / publish helper around tape:// sites and containers. Still verify domains and any wallet prompts before signing.
## Boundaries / non-claims
- Explicitly not an official gateway (per author / guide).
- Not a substitute for verifying contract or container ownership yourself.
