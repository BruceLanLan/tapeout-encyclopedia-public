## Definition
**TapeWeb** (web.tapeout.link) is listed in beginner-guide ch.8 as a network-wide on-chain site viewer (page title “TapeWeb · TapeOut 全网链上站点查看器”).

## How it works
Use it to browse / discover tape:// sites. Still verify domains and any wallet prompts before opening third-party destinations or connecting a wallet.

## Boundaries / non-claims
- Not an official gateway.
- Catalog and reachability are third-party-maintained and may lag or break.
