## Definition

**REF** means reusing someone else’s existing circuit as a black box inside a new design.

## How it works

Design tooling may allow references for modularity; the beginner guide states **current mining rules disallow** REF circuits in PoD. Follow the latest official rules.

## Boundaries / non-claims

- “Canvas allows REF” is not the same as “eligible for mining”.
